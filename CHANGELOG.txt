MicroSlideStitcher changelog
============================

0.8.1 — 2026-10-24
- A JPEG copy for sharing is saved next to every TIFF or PNG panorama (same name, .jpg; also for
  extra pieces). --jpeg-copy no turns it off. A 3566 x 8501 px scan: 43.5 MB TIFF, 3.5 MB JPEG.
- --jpeg-quality (1-100, default 90) sets the quality of the copy and of a .jpg --output
  (previously fixed at 95).
- GUI: "JPEG copy for sharing" setting, "JPEG quality" under Advanced settings, and a
  "Save as JPEG..." button in the Panorama tab that converts the finished panorama - or one from
  an earlier run - without stitching again. The Panorama tab buttons are now in two rows.
- Panoramas larger than JPEG allows (65,500 px per side) get a reduced JPEG and a warning; the
  TIFF/PNG stays full size.
- The unsupported-extension error now lists .jpg and .jpeg.

0.8.0 — 2026-10-24
- Content-based reconnection: when the chain still breaks into pieces (the slide moved too fast,
  or every frame of a turn was blurred), every frame of every piece is described once by its
  SIFT features and votes for the frames of the other pieces it resembles, so frames showing
  the same tissue are found regardless of when they were recorded - the row above, or the
  same strip on the way back. Each candidate must pass the normal matcher and the full overlap
  check. Two pieces are merged only when at least two links, using different frames on both
  sides, agree on where one piece sits relative to the other. --no-reconnect turns it off;
  every candidate, link and merge is written to reconnect.json and summary.json.
- After a reconnection all frame positions are refined together (shift-only bundle
  adjustment, also in single-sweep mode), so overlapping passes line up.

0.7.1 — 2026-10-24
- Frames are now taken from a video by sharpness and movement (--frame-selection motion, the
  new default): every frame is read once at small size, sharpness is measured separately for
  horizontal and vertical detail on the original frame (before CLAHE, which inflated the score
  of blurred frames), each frame is compared with the sharp frames around it, and the sharpest
  non-blurred frame of each stretch of movement is kept. Blurred frames - including motion blur
  from a moving slide - are skipped. --frame-selection fixed restores 0.7.0 sampling. Every
  decoded frame and the reason it was kept or skipped is written to frame_selection.csv.
- Every merge of two frames - consecutive, bridged, row turn, segment rejoin and loop closure -
  must now pass one overlap check that is never relaxed: at least --min-overlap (10 %) of the
  frame shared horizontally AND vertically, the matched points spread over at least
  --min-overlap-spread (15 %) of the shared width and height, and the two frames correlating at
  least --min-overlap-agreement (0.60) inside the shared area. Previously a rejoin could join
  frames with no overlap at all (translation up to 2 frames).
- Bundle adjustment refines shifts only. The similarity model could shrink frames to lower its
  own error score while making the panorama worse, and the OpenCV adjuster could not be stopped
  at the time limit; neither is used any more.
- GUI: "Frames taken from video" and "Minimum overlap" settings; the status line describes the
  frame selection actually used.

0.7.0 — 2026-08-25
- Added --scan-mode {sweep,serpentine} (default sweep, byte-identical to 0.6.0) and global bundle
  adjustment behind --bundle-adjust / --no-bundle-adjust, with --ba-max-iterations,
  --ba-max-seconds, --ba-max-pairs, --ba-min-overlap, --serpentine-min-overlap and
  --serpentine-turn-translation-frac.
- Added non-adjacent (loop-closure) match discovery: pairs are shortlisted by predicted overlap
  from the chained placement, then really feature-matched through the existing matcher and the
  existing inlier/scale/rotation/reprojection gates, with a hard cap instead of an O(n^2) search.
- Added joint refinement of all frame transforms over every verified correspondence, one frame
  held fixed as the gauge, with a Huber-robust cost. OpenCV's detail bundle adjusters are tried
  first; a numpy-only Levenberg-Marquardt is the fallback, so scipy stays out of the bundle.
- The chained solution is kept, and a panorama is still written, whenever the refinement fails,
  hits its iteration or wall-clock cap, or does not reduce the robust cost.
- Serpentine mode tolerates the direction reversal and step-down at a row turn via a wider
  translation allowance for that one pair; all other geometric gates are unchanged.
- GUI: "Scan type" control with plain-language options, an advanced bundle-adjustment checkbox
  and iteration cap, and new results rows; report.html and summary.json report pairs found and
  accepted, iterations, and reprojection error before versus after.
- When bundle adjustment changes the geometry, the transforms actually used are written to
  diag/transforms_bundle_adjusted.json; transforms.json still holds the chained solution.
